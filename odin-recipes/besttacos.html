Best Tacos

Enjoy these delicious and easy-to-make tacos!

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 tsp chili powder
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. In a large skillet, cook the ground beef until browned.
  2. In a small bowl, whisk together the chili powder, cumin, paprika, salt, and pepper.
  3. Pour the spice mix over the beef and simmer for 5-10 minutes until heated through.
