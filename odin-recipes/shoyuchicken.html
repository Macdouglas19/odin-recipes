Shoyu Chicken

A delicious and easy-to-make shoyu chicken recipe.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb chicken breast, cut into bite-sized pieces
  • 2 tbsp shoyu (soy sauce)
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp ginger, grated

Instructions

  1. In a large skillet, cook the chicken pieces until browned.
  2. In a small bowl, whisk together the shoyu, rice vinegar, sugar, garlic, and ginger.
  3. Pour the sauce over the chicken and simmer for 5-10 minutes until heated through.
