German Chocolate Cake

A delicious German chocolate cake recipe.

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 cup butter, softened
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 cup German chocolate pieces

Instructions:

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. In a large bowl, cream together the butter and sugar.
  3. Beat in the eggs one at a time.
  4. Alternately add the flour and milk to the creamed mixture.
  5. Stir in the vanilla extract.
  6. Fold in the German chocolate pieces.
  7. Pour the batter into a greased 9x13 inch baking pan.
  8. Bake for 30-35 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
